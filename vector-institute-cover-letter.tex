\vspace{0.3em}
\noindent
I am writing to apply for membership as a Vector Institute Faculty Member. I am an Associate Professor with tenure in the Department of Computer Science at the National University of Singapore and a nominee for the Canada Impact+ Research Chair at the University of Toronto Mississauga in Mathematical and Computational Sciences. I am enthusiastic about contributing to Vector's research community, particularly in ML security---a field I have helped shape over the past decade.

\noindent
My ML security research has been foundational. I was among the first to demonstrate \emph{data poisoning} attacks in neural network training (ACSAC 2016); this work is cited in the OWASP ML Security Top Ten. I served as Area Chair for ``Machine Learning Security'' at ACM CCS 2020--21, and have developed formal ML security abstractions---geometric characterisations, causal models, and post-quantum formulations for model inversion. Most recently, my group achieved the first computationally tractable defences against data poisoning with provable guarantees. I am extending this toward AI-assisted migration to secure languages (including C-to-Rust for large codebases) and secure hardware abstractions (CAPSTONE).

\noindent
Beyond ML security, my work has had substantial real-world impact. I designed the security architecture for the Google Chrome extensions platform (deployed 2010, billions of users), language-based defences for cross-site scripting that formed the basis of the Closure compiler and Trusted Types, and web sandboxing integrated into Dropbox. In decentralized systems, I introduced practical blockchain sharding (Elastico, CCS 2016), co-founded Zilliqa (billion-dollar valuation, 2018), and established DeFi smart-contract auditing with Oyente and MAIAN. The ``verifier dilemma'' (CCS 2015) received the ACM CCS Test of Time Award (2025). I directed NUS's Crystal Center for blockchain research (2018--2025), and my PhD graduates hold faculty positions at Georgia Tech and ETH Zurich. Recognitions include MIT TR35 ``Top 10 Innovators'' Asia (2017) and the Google Security \& Privacy Researcher Award (2018).

\noindent
As a Vector Faculty Member, I plan to advance ML security along three lines: training-free defences for adversarial and privacy-leaking outputs; secure reasoning and information flow for AI agents; and verifiable AI-assisted migration to secure languages. I look forward to collaborating with Vector researchers and contributing to Canada's leadership in trustworthy AI.

\vspace{0.3em}
\noindent
Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to the possibility of joining the Vector Institute.
